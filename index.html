      How to play

      The rules

      Each fleet has a Man-o'-War (5 squares), Galleon (4), Frigate (3), Brigantine (3) and Sloop (2) on a 10 by 10 chart. Take turns firing one shot at a square. A hit names the ship you struck, and a sinking is announced. You can't fire at the same square twice. Sink all five enemy ships to win.

      Gambit rules

      Your captain has one Gambit per battle, ready from your first turn. Using it is your turn, except Crow's Nest, which is free: you still fire afterwards.

      • Broadside: fire at any 3 untried squares.
      • Powder Keg: a plus-shaped blast on open water, away from ships you've already found.
      • Crow's Nest: learn how many ship squares lie in a 3 by 3 area.
      • Ghost Ship: move one of your ships to untouched water and repair one hit.

      Controls

      • Chart: click to fire, G uses your Gambit, right-click or Esc cancels aiming.
      • Tab boards your ship: W A S D walk, mouse to look, 1–5 change ship, Q opens the sea chart to fire.
      • K comfort mode (less shake, steady horizon), M mute, F full screen, Esc menu.
